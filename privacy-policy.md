# **Privacy Policy**

**Effective Date:** 08/01/2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Daily Current Affairs** (“we”, “our”, “the app”) collects, uses, and protects your information.

By using this app, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### a) Account Information (Google Login)

We use **Google Sign-In** for authentication.

When you log in using Google, we may receive:

* Your name
* Email address
* Profile photo (if provided by Google)

We **do not** access your Google password.

---

### b) App Usage Data

When you use the app, we may store:

* Test attempts and scores
* Dates of attempts
* Answers selected during tests
* Streak and progress history

This data is used **only to provide app features**, such as history, revision, and analytics.

---

### c) Guest Usage

If you use the app without logging in:

* Your test progress may be stored **temporarily on your device only**
* This data is saved locally and is **not uploaded** until you choose to log in

## 3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

* Authenticate users via Google Login
* Save test results and progress
* Display history, revision, and performance analytics
* Improve app functionality and user experience

We **do not** use your data for advertising or profiling.

## 4. Data Storage & Security

All user data is stored securely using **Supabase**, a managed backend platform.

Security measures include:

* Secure authentication
* Encrypted connections (HTTPS)
* Database access controls

While we take reasonable measures to protect your data, **no system is 100% secure**, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 5. Data Sharing

We **do not sell, rent, or trade** your personal data.

Your data may be processed by:

* **Google** (for authentication)
* **Supabase** (for secure data storage)

Only the **minimum necessary data** is shared with these services to operate the app.
